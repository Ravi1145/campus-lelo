# Competitor teardown (15 Oct 2026)

Method: opened each page in a browser (narrow, phone-like viewport) and read the page structure with a script
(headings, links, buttons, forms, JSON-LD types, fixed elements). Numbers below are what the page showed that day.
Figures like "1,479 links" are counts from the page, not rankings or traffic.

## CollegeDekho (collegedekho.com)

Homepage: height 8,522 px, 701 links, 210 images. Headline "Right Guidance, Bright Future". Sections: Online Degrees,
"Choosing the right college can be confusing", "Praised by the media", Find the Perfect College (form with name, phone,
email, stream), Top Colleges Recommended For You, Our Achievements, student testimonials, "Get in touch with our expert
counsellors". Primary buttons: "Find Your College", "Get Job Ready Degree", "Get Free Counselling". Tool links: JEE Main
rank predictor and college predictor, NEET rank and college predictor, DU predictor, state counselling pages.
Header and bottom navigation are fixed. A Google rating badge sits under the hero.
JSON-LD on homepage: WebSite, Organization.

College page (Chitkara University): 23,626 px tall, about 6,100 words. Headings: About, Highlights, Student preferred colleges,
Courses and Fees, Admission, Admission dates, Entrance exams accepted, Admission process, Dates and events, Placements (with
"LPA" mentioned 15 times), Top recruiters, Reviews and rating (45 reviews), FAQs, Similar colleges. Named writer byline
("Amit Singh - Content Writer"). Course tabs by degree (BE, MBA, BBA, BCA, BPharm and others). Buttons: Download Brochure,
Shortlist, Write a Review, Get Free Counselling. Tables: Events and Date; Course Name and Exam Accepted; Particulars and
Highlights. No visible source line for figures. JSON-LD: CollegeOrUniversity, EducationalOrganization, FAQPage,
BreadcrumbList, Article, Person, ItemList, ImageGallery.

## Collegedunia (collegedunia.com)

Homepage: 6,078 px, 413 links. Entry points: Find 250+ exams, Select your study goal, Explore programs, Ranking, Find colleges,
College Predictor, Compare Colleges, Course Finder, Top Universities, Latest News. Menu also lists: Write a Review, Read College
Reviews, Admission Alerts, Education Loan, Ask a Question, Test Series, Practice Questions, Scholarship, Articles, Top Coupons,
Study and Work Abroad. Form: email, mobile, course. JSON-LD on homepage: Organization only.

College page (Amity University Noida): 23,666 px, about 6,650 words. Tabs: Info, Courses and Fees, Admission, Reviews,
Department, CutOff, Placement, Ranking, Gallery, Scholarship, Faculty, News and Articles, Hostel, Affiliated Colleges, Community,
College Compare, Q&A. Shows 3,097 reviews overall and "221 Answered Questions". Sections include "A vs B vs C" comparison,
scholarship table (scholarship, eligibility, benefit), ranking table (body, category, 2025 rank), admission-dates table
(programme, application status, key date), fees table (course, duration, eligibility, total fees). Attribution to
"Collegedunia Team". No source line for figures. JSON-LD: WebPage, FAQPage, Organization, CollegeOrUniversity, Article,
UserComments.

## Shiksha (shiksha.com)

Homepage: 6,030 px, 1,479 links, only about 1,590 words. The home page is an MBA-led mega-menu: top MBA colleges in India,
Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Kerala; MBA, Executive, Distance, Online, Part-time; about a dozen specialisations;
exams CAT, CMAT, SNAP, XAT, MAT. Predictor links: CAT percentile predictor, IIM and non-IIM call predictor, CAT college
predictor, MAH CET college predictor, XAT call predictor, IIFT predictor, NMAT predictor. College review links for IIMs,
FMS, SP Jain, MDI. JSON-LD on homepage: Organization.

MBA list page "Best MBA Colleges in Pune": 14,853 px, about 1,370 words of text outside cards. Headline sections: Highlights
2026, NIRF 2025, Basic eligibility, Entrance exams, Private colleges, ROI, MBA without entrance exam, Low fees, Government
colleges, then college cards. City filter list (Pune, Mumbai, Nagpur, Nashik and many others) and "Application Open Now".
JSON-LD: BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, WebPage, WebSite, ItemList, Article, Organization.

## Careers360 (careers360.com)

Homepage: 7,469 px, 1,353 links, only about 680 words. Product groups: Counselling, Data, Prediction, Online Courses and
Certifications, Other products. Top exams (JEE Main, JEE Advanced, GATE, KIITEE, LPUNEST, MET, SRMJEE, VITEEE and many state
exams), then predictors and ebooks. Heavy on predictors (JEE, TS EAMCET, MHT CET, KCET, GUJCET, AP EAMCET, WBJEE, COMEDK,
GATE, NEET) and rank boosters. JSON-LD on homepage: Organization, WebSite.

## CollegeVidya (collegevidya.com)

Online and distance degrees. Homepage 14,094 px, about 1,525 words. Headline: "Compare 100+ Online Universities on 30+
Factors". Sections: advisor trained by experts, Explore courses and colleges, "Will I Pass My Online Degree?" quiz,
Learning Management System, University Reviews, Calculate ROI, "Why choose online?", EMI messaging ("Put that money to
work"), learner stories, press mentions, doubts section, "Checklist I Wish I Had Before Enrolling", a "Meet Sara" assistant,
and a story block on wrong versus right degree. "Compare N now" buttons per programme (for example "Online Global MBA: 37").
Fixed bottom bar: Home, Offers, Tools, Profile, Ask Sara. Sign-in button. Named expert on the page (Dr. Vijender Singh,
Education Expert). JSON-LD: WebPage, MobileApplication, FAQPage, ItemList, WebSite, EducationalOrganization, Organization.

## KollegeApply (kollegeapply.com)

Very light homepage: 707 px, about 480 words. Headline "Explore Top Colleges, Exams, Results and More". Sections: Explore
programs, Top Colleges in India 2026 (IIMA, IIT Bombay, AIIMS Delhi, NLSIU, IIT Delhi, IIM Calcutta, Hindu College). Buttons:
Login, Predict Your All Admission Chances, Brochure, College Predictor, Apply Now. Fixed: top stream and course bar, a banner
ad ("Launch Your Allied Health Career Without NEET"), and "Ask Orbit AI" at bottom-right. JSON-LD: Organization, WebSite,
BreadcrumbList, EducationalOrganization, WebPage, FAQPage, Article.
