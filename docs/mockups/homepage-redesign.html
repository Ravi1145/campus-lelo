What students ask us most

Short answers, with links to the pages that go deeper.

Is counselling really free? +

The first phone call is free. A one-to-one video session costs ₹199, and the price is shown before you book.

Where do your dates and cut-offs come from? +

From each institute's or exam body's own page. Every line links to it and shows the day we checked.

Is the psychometric test a real psychometric test? +

It is a guidance test in that style, not a standardised or clinical one. Use it to start a conversation.

Do you show placement packages? +

Only when a source backs the number. Until then we leave it out rather than guess.
