Questions we get a lot

short answers, promise.

Q.Is counselling really free?

The first phone call is. A one-to-one video session is ₹199, and you see the price before you book.

Q.Where do your dates and cut-offs come from?

From the college's or exam body's own page. Every line links to it and shows the day we checked.

Q.Is the psychometric test a real one?

It is a guidance test in that style, not a standardised or clinical one. Use it to start a conversation with someone you trust.

Q.Why no placement packages?

Because we can't point to a source for them yet. We would rather leave a gap than guess.
