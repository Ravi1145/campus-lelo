# College search portals CampusLelo competes with

Checked 15 Oct 2026 with two web searches. Confidence: "Seen in results" means the name and a role
appeared in a source. "Not verified here" means I know the name but found nothing in these searches,
so check the site yourself before relying on it. Traffic and size figures are what the sites say about
themselves, not independent numbers.

| Site | What it does | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| CollegeDekho (collegedekho.com) | College database plus a common application and counselling. Self-reported 35,000+ colleges. | Seen in results |
| Collegedunia (collegedunia.com) | College database with student reviews, exam news, rankings pages. | Seen in results |
| Shiksha (shiksha.com) | Run by Info Edge. Courses, colleges, exams, common application form. | Seen in results |
| Careers360 (careers360.com) | Exam news, college lists, "career after 12th" guidance, Q&A. | Seen in results |
| GetMyUni (getmyuni.com) | College and exam portal; appears as a CollegeDekho competitor in third-party lists. | Seen in results (thin) |
| Book My College | Listed with the above as a site that shows fees, reviews and admission process. | Seen in results (thin) |

## What this means for CampusLelo

- These sites win on size: tens of thousands of college pages, reviews, and years of links. Do not try to beat
  them on head terms like "MBA colleges in Pune" soon.
- Many show fees, placements and cut-offs without a source, and their figures often disagree with each other.
  CampusLelo's sourced pages are the gap to compete on (see docs/keywords/RANK-TARGETS.md).
- Online-degree sellers (CollegeVidya, KollegeApply) mostly earn commission from admissions. CampusLelo does
  not need to copy that model; free counselling plus the paid video session is a different position.
