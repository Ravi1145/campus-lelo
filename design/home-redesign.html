Questions students ask us

Quick answers

What is CampusLelo?

CampusLelo is a college and MBA admissions guide for Pune, Noida, Delhi NCR, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand. It shows fees, entrance exams and cut-offs that are checked against the institute's own pages, and offers a free counselling call.

Is the counselling really free?

Yes. The first counselling call is free. We contact you only after you tick the consent box, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

Where do your fees and cut-offs come from?

From each institute's official page or brochure, and from exam bodies' own sites. Every figure shows its source and the date we checked it. If we cannot confirm a figure, we leave it out.

Can I get an MBA in Delhi without CAT?

Some programmes list other exams such as XAT, GMAT, CMAT or MAT. FMS, IIFT, IIT Delhi DMS and MDI use CAT for most applicants. See the without-CAT guide for the full picture.

Is a PGDM the same as an MBA?

Not automatically. An MBA is a university degree; a PGDM is a diploma from an AICTE-approved institute, and equivalence depends on the programme. Ask the institute for its equivalence letter if it matters to you.
